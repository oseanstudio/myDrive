# myDrive

myDrive helps you move files between your computers and keep your folders up to date.

- Keep a project folder up to date on your laptop and desktop.
- Send a folder of photos or a web link to another device with Blip.
- Share with a nearby Apple device through AirDrop. Linux uses optional Owly, and Windows 11 uses the experimental WirDrop companion with a supported adapter. [AirDrop setup](docs/AIRDROP.md)
- Use **SMB Drives** to browse your desktop’s shared folders from your laptop as if they were local. The files stay on your desktop, and you can open them without copying or syncing the folder first.
- Use **Cloud Drives** to back up a project to Dropbox, OneDrive, or Google Drive and recover earlier copies.
